Your safety comes first

Injectable treatments are performed by registered nurses and physicians, under the direction of our medical director. Every treatment starts with a consultation.

  • Licensed injectors

    Injectables are performed by registered nurses and physicians trained in aesthetic medicine.

  • Medical director oversight

    Our treatment protocols are set and reviewed by our medical director.

  • Consultation first

    Your medical history, medications and goals are reviewed before any treatment is booked.

  • Sterile, single-use supplies

    Needles, cannulas and treatment tips are single-use, and every room is cleaned between clients.

  • Approved products

    We use products approved for use in Canada, bought from authorized distributors.

  • Follow-up visit

    We book a check-in about two weeks after injectables to review your result with you.
