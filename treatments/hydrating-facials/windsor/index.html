Planning around life in Windsor

Humid Windsor summers leave skin oilier and more congested, while furnace-dry winters leave it dull and tight. A skin plan here usually shifts with the seasons: clarifying treatments in summer, and resurfacing and hydration once the weather cools.

The riverfront trail, backyard pools and cottage weekends mean summer sun exposure adds up for Windsor clients, which is why sunscreen comes up at every visit.
