How pricing works

Most treatments are priced per area, per syringe, per session or as a package for a series. The price that suits you depends on what you want to change, how many areas are involved and how your skin or face responds, which is why we confirm it at your consultation. You'll always know the price before treatment begins.

Not sure where to start? Try the treatment finder, read our frequently asked questions or simply book a consultation.

Prices in CAD, before applicable taxes. Injectable treatments are performed by registered nurses and physicians, under the direction of our medical director. Every treatment starts with a consultation.
