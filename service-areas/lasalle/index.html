Med spa treatments in LaSalle

LaSalle clients come to us for subtle injectables, skin treatments that fit around family life and laser courses planned for the months when they are out of the sun.

LaSalle has grown quickly into a town of young families and busy professionals, many of them commuting into Windsor. Clients from the Front Road area, the subdivisions around the Vollmer Complex and the newer neighbourhoods to the south come to us for treatments that look natural and fit around a full schedule.

Life along the Detroit River means sun on the water, boating and long summer evenings outdoors, so sun spots and uneven tone are common concerns. Below you'll find every treatment we offer, with notes for LaSalle clients.
